## 2. How to read
- Search: `https://www.kupi.cz/hledej?f=<word>`; each product shows "Regular price", then per chain: price, pack, discount, CZK per 100 g, "ends tomorrow" or dates, "In the flyer".
- A chain page: `https://www.kupi.cz/slevy/<chain>` (Norma showed "no products on sale in your location" for Prague on 19/10).
- Label `leaflet`; add "via kupi.cz" in `nutrition_source`/notes.
