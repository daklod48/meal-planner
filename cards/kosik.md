# Košík.cz (card key: `kosik`)

_Card written 2026-10-19 (session 2)._

## 1. In one line
Online-only supermarket with the full assortment, prices and nutrition on every product page; no connector, so Claude reads it in Chrome. Also sells the Delmart range.

## 2. Where it is near the saved places
No shop. Delivers to both places: the Košík address check said "We deliver to the address you specify" for Chemická 955 (Praha 4 – Kunratice) and Roztylská 2321/19 (Praha 4 – Chodov) on 19/10/2026. Row `D-kosik`.

## 3. Prices online
- **Search:** `https://www.kosik.cz/vyhledavani?search=<words>`. Each tile: price, price per kg, multi-buy price ("from 2 pcs"), product link `/p<number>-<slug>`.
- **Product page:** price and CZK/kg at the top; open the tab "Složení" (Composition) for the nutrition table.
- Chrome may show the page translated into English; the numbers are the same.
- **Label:** `online`.

## 4. Loyalty price
No card key in the profile. Klub VELKOvýhod: after one order of at least 1,500 CZK, 30 days of benefits including free delivery and discounts on selected brands (Košík, n.d.-b). Multi-buy prices ("od 2 ks") apply to everybody: record them as a note, not as a card price.

## 5. Nutrition
Product page, tab "Složení", table "Nutriční hodnoty (na 100 g)": label `exact`.

## 6. Delivery
- Both places, 19/10/2026: minimum purchase 749 CZK; free delivery above 1,199 CZK; below that the fee of the chosen time slot is shown at checkout (Košík address check). Time windows of 30 or 60 min from 11:30 the same day were offered.
- Express 60 or 90 min "for a small surcharge" in selected regions (Košík, n.d.-a).

## 7. Cart
Chrome only, on his account; account check by the name in the account menu. Not tested in session 2 (session 3).

## 8. Pitfalls
"Tvaroh odtučněný" search also returns soft and BIO tvaroh with different protein; check the name and the per-100 g protein. Multi-buy price is for 2 packs.
